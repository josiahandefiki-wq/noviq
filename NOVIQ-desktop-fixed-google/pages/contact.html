FAQs

Common Questions

How do I start a project?

Send us the form above with a few details about your business and what you would like to achieve. We will reply with clear next steps.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and type of work. We agree a realistic schedule with you after our first conversation.

Can you work with a small budget?

Yes. We focus on small businesses and will recommend a sensible starting point that fits your budget and goals.

What should I prepare?

It helps to know your main goal, who your customers are and any examples of brands or websites you like. If you do not have these yet, we can help you work them out.
