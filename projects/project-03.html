Google Local Services Ads — Our Approach

How NOVIQ sets up and manages Google Local Services Ads so local businesses show up Google Guaranteed for the searches that matter.

The brief

For a local service business, showing up with a phone number and a strong rating the moment someone searches “electrician near me” matters more than a clever slogan. Google Local Services Ads put a business at the top of local search with a Google Guaranteed badge, real reviews and one tap to call or book — but only once the account is set up and verified correctly. This is how we get it right.

Our approach

  • We take the business through Google's background-check and licence verification, since the Guaranteed badge only appears once that's approved.
  • We build out the service list and service area precisely, so ads only show for jobs the business can actually take on.
  • We set the budget against a measurable goal — booked jobs or calls — and run it as pay-per-lead rather than pay-per-click.
  • We connect call tracking and booking data back to the ads, so it's clear which leads turned into real work.
  • We keep reviews and response time current, because both affect ranking and trust as much as the ad itself.

Deliverables

  • Google Local Services Ads account setup and verification
  • Service list and service area configuration
  • Call and lead tracking setup
  • Ongoing budget and performance monitoring
